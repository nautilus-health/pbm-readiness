An invitation to the 2027 Expert Review Panel

Dear colleague,

You're here because you know something we need. You may have negotiated a PBM contract line by line, defended a committee's decision under ERISA, priced a specialty drug pipeline, or sat across from a broker and asked how they get paid. Experience like yours is what turns a guide from correct into useful.

CAA 2026 and the DOL's PBM Fee Disclosure Rule change what plan sponsors must ask for, review, and act on. These fifteen documents turn those rules into plain steps for the people who carry the duty: fiduciary committees, HR and finance leaders, and the advisors who serve them. Nautilus publishes them free. What this panel tells us will shape how plan sponsors and advisors are taught to protect the employees and families who depend on their health plans.

Contribute in whatever way suits you:

  • Mark up a full document with tracked changes.
  • Comment on the one section you know best.
  • Suggest language we should adopt, word for word.
  • Point us to a case, regulation, or source we should cite.
  • Tell us what's missing, wrong, or unclear.
  • Put the PBM Rule Navigator to the test with the hardest question you hear from clients.

One sharp comment is as welcome as a full markup. Every submission is read.

With your permission, we'll list you by name as a member of the 2027 Expert Review Panel in the published editions. You choose how you're credited when you send your review.

Comments are due December 19, 2026. The final editions publish in January 2027. Thank you for lending your judgment to this work.

Steve Ditto Program Director, PBM Fiduciary Standards Initiative Nautilus Health Institute

How it works

  1. Download any document below. Each one arrives as an editable Word file, always the latest draft. For the PBM Rule Navigator, choose Launch and try it.
  2. Mark it up with tracked changes and comments, or jot your thoughts down as you read. Review one document or all fifteen.
  3. Come back here and choose Return review. Upload your file or type your comments, and tell us who you are.

You'll get an email confirming we received it. Questions, or rather talk it through? Write to steve.ditto@nautilushealth.org.

Reviewing more than one? Open the shared Drive folder and choose Download to get all fifteen in a single zip file. Comments that cover several documents, or none in particular, are welcome too.
