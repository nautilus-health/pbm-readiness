# PBM Rule Readiness Check

A free tool from Nautilus Health Institute. Plan sponsors and advisors answer seven questions and see what the DOL final PBM rule (29 CFR 2550.408b-22) and the Consolidated Appropriations Act (CAA) of 2026 mean for their plan.

**Status:** v0.3 preview. Intake and results are live. Flow: ask a question first (up to 5 free, answered from an approved knowledge pack), or answer 7 quick questions for deadlines and an action plan; the plan details tailor every answer and show above it. The full report is emailed and downloaded as a PDF (which unlocks 5 more questions). Visitors can rate each answer. Questions and email are switched on with `?api=` until counsel signs off.
