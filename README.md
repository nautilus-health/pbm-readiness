# PBM Rule Readiness Check

A free tool from Nautilus Health Institute. Plan sponsors and advisors answer seven questions and see what the DOL final PBM rule (29 CFR 2550.408b-22) and the Consolidated Appropriations Act (CAA) of 2026 mean for their plan.

**Status:** v0.3 preview. Intake and results are live. Flow: 7 questions one at a time, results with no email, up to 5 questions answered from an approved knowledge pack, then the full report emailed and downloaded as a PDF (which unlocks 5 more questions). Questions and email are switched on with `?api=` until counsel signs off. See [DEPLOY.md](DEPLOY.md). Logic pending counsel review.

Live: https://nautilus-health.github.io/pbm-readiness/

General information, not legal advice.
